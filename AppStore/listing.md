## Description (4,000 max)
Trophy Rooms is a home for everything you've played, everything you own, and everything you're still chasing.

Most trackers stop at "played it." This one keeps the whole room: the library you're working through, the physical shelf behind you, and the hours you actually put in.

YOUR LIBRARY, HONESTLY SORTED
Every game you play in one place, from retro classics to this year's releases. Give each one a status — backlog, playing, paused, completed, dropped — and see the pile shrink. Filter by platform, sort how you like, and jump back into whatever you left unfinished.

THE SHELF, PROPERLY CATALOGED
Physical collectors get first-class treatment. Record region, condition, and whether a copy is complete-in-box or still sealed. Track boxes, manuals, and extras. Multi-game compilations and bundles are handled properly instead of being flattened into one entry. Keep a buylist for what's next and a sell list for what's on its way out.

A PLAY JOURNAL THAT KEEPS YOU HONEST
Log what you played and for how long in a single tap. Daily entries build a streak worth protecting, and weekly and all-time totals show where your hours really went.

ACHIEVEMENTS AND TROPHIES
Some games have achievement lists you can work through. You tick each one off yourself as you earn it, and nothing syncs from your consoles or PC stores. Finish a list and the trophy lands in your room. Only a small set of games has lists so far.

BUILT ON A REAL CATALOG
Search more than 28,000 games across 38 platforms. Cover art and release dates are already filled in, so you can add a game without typing anything.

SEE WHERE YOU STAND
Leaderboards and a community activity feed show what everyone else is finishing.

Free to use. Sign in with Apple, Google, or email — and delete your account and data from inside the app whenever you want.
