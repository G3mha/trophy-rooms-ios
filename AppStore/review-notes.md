## Notes (paste into the Notes box)

Trophy Rooms is a free tracker for video game collections. An account is
required because every screen shows personal data — the games you are playing,
the physical copies you own, and your play history.

DEMO ACCOUNT
Email: appreview@trophyrooms.org
Password: <paste from 1Password>

This account is pre-populated with a game library, a physical collection, play
journal entries, and earned trophies, so no screen appears empty during review.

SIGN-IN OPTIONS
Sign in with Apple, Continue with Google, and email + password are all
supported. Sign in with Apple is offered alongside the third-party option in
accordance with guideline 4.8.

ACCOUNT DELETION (guideline 5.1.1(v))
1. Tap the profile icon in the top-right corner of any main tab
2. The Account sheet opens
3. Tap "Delete Account", then confirm with "Delete Everything"

This permanently deletes the account, the sign-in identity, and all collection,
library, trophy, and play journal data. It is not a deactivation.

ADDITIONAL NOTES
- Game titles, cover art, and release dates are reference data in our catalog.
  Users add their own ownership and progress records on top of that catalog.
- A network connection is required. The app talks to our API at
  api.trophyrooms.org.
- There are no in-app purchases, no advertising, and no third-party analytics
  or tracking SDKs.
- The app supports iPhone and iPad.
